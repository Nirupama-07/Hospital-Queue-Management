Frequently Asked Questions

Orthopedics is the medical specialty that focuses on diagnosing, treating, and preventing conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, tendons, and the spine.

You should consult an orthopedic specialist if you experience persistent joint pain, back pain, fractures, swelling, limited movement, sports injuries, or difficulty walking.
